Rebirth

0 RP

Rebirthing resets energy and upgrades. Each rebirth gives +20% to everything and Rebirth Points. You need rebirths to ascend, and ascending resets them.

Rebirth Shop

Rebirth once to unlock the shop.

Ascension

0 AP

Ascending resets your rebirths, Rebirth Points, rebirth shop levels, energy and upgrades. In return you get Ascension Points and a permanent multiplier.

Ascension Shop

Ascend once to unlock the shop.

Challenges

Ascend once to unlock challenges.

Starting a challenge resets your run like an ascension (no Ascension Points). Reach the ascension requirement under the challenge rules, then complete it for a permanent reward.

Codes
