Rebirth

0 RP

Rebirthing resets energy and upgrades. Each rebirth gives +20% to everything and Rebirth Points. You need rebirths to ascend, and ascending resets them.

Rebirth Shop

Rebirth once to unlock the shop.

Shop levels can be sold back for 90% of what you paid.

Ascension

0 AP

Current tier: Core · Next tier: Stellar at 5 ascensions.

Ascending resets your rebirths, Rebirth Points, rebirth shop levels, energy and upgrades. In return you get Ascension Points and a permanent multiplier.

Ascension Shop

Ascend once to unlock the shop.

Shop levels can be sold back for 90% of what you paid.

Challenges

Ascend once to unlock challenges.

Starting a challenge resets your run like an ascension (no Ascension Points). Reach the ascension requirement under the challenge rules, then complete it for the reward.

Codes

Codes pop up now and then while you play. You may see ones you already used, and codes can't be reused. An Auto-Claim upgrade in the Rebirth Shop can redeem them for you.
